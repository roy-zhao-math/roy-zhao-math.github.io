Tsinghua Number Theory Learning Seminar: Canonical Integral Models of Shimura Varieties

Spring 2025

This is the website for the Tsinghua Number Theory Learning Seminar. This is a weekly seminar intended to introduce participants to Shimura varieties and cover their canonical integral models. Each week, a speaker will present on a topic. Here is the outline (PDF).
If you would like to be added to the seminar mailing list, send an email to me.
The seminar is scheduled to meet 1:15-3:15 PM Thursdays.
